>  * **Masonry (Brick):** Parallel diagonal lines with a 'stippled' or 'dotted' texture.
>  * **CMU (Block):** Hollow rectangles or diagonal cross-hatching (45^\circ).
>  * **Wood Studs:** Empty cavities with a single 'X' or parallel lines with no hatching.
>  * **Concrete Slabs:** Fine stippling (dots) and a solid boundary line."
> 
### 4. Geometric Pattern Recognition (OpenCV Integration)
While LLMs (like GPT-4o or Gemini) are great at reasoning, **OpenCV** is better at math. Use a hybrid approach in Replit:
 * **OpenCV:** Use cv2.findContours and HoughLines to identify long straight lines (walls) and closed polygons (slabs).
 * **AI Vision:** The AI then "tags" those polygons (e.g., "This polygon is the Brick Veneer area").
 * **The Calculation:** Once the AI identifies the shape, the Python backend calculates the area precisely based on the scale calibration.
### 5. The "Professionalism" Features (The Competitive Edge)

 * **The Deductions Engine:** Ensure the AI automatically subtracts "Openings" (Windows and Doors) from the square footage. A "rookie" AI will calculate a solid wall; a "pro" AI sees the window and deducts 15\text{ sq ft} of brick.
